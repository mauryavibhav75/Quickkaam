QuickKaam operates solely as a connecting platform between workers and service seekers. We do not employ workers, control work execution, or dictate how services should be performed. Workers operate independently and are not employees or agents of QuickKaam.

2 Worker Responsibilities

  • Provide accurate, truthful, and up-to-date personal and professional information during registration.
  • Perform all accepted work with honesty, professionalism, and adherence to safety standards.
  • Communicate clearly with users regarding work scope, timeline, and any expected charges before commencing work.
  • Maintain ethical conduct and respect user privacy at all times.

3 Payments & Charges

Important: QuickKaam does not process, manage, or guarantee any payments. All payment arrangements are the sole responsibility of workers and users.

Workers are fully and independently responsible for:

  • Proper execution and completion of accepted work.

QuickKaam does not monitor, supervise, or track work progress or quality.

5 Request Acceptance Policy

  • Workers have full discretion to accept or reject any service request based on their availability and capability.
  • Once a request is accepted, the worker is obligated to communicate promptly with the user and make a genuine effort to complete the work.
  • Repeated acceptance and abandonment of requests may result in account suspension or termination.

6 User Information & Privacy

  • Workers receive user contact details and location information strictly for work-related communication and service delivery.
  • Misuse of user information for purposes other than the accepted service request is strictly prohibited.
