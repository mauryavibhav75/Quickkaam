📋 Terms & Conditions for Users

Welcome to QUICKKAAM User Platform!
By using this platform, you agree to comply with the following terms and conditions. Please read them carefully before raising any service requests.

1 Platform Role

QuickKaam is only a service-connecting platform. We help users find workers based on location and work type. We do not provide services directly and do not supervise or control any work.

2 User Responsibilities

Users must provide correct personal details, including name, mobile number, and location. Users are responsible for clearly explaining their work requirements to the worker. Any communication with the worker happens directly between user and worker.

3 Payments & Transactions

QuickKaam does not collect, process, or track any payments. Payment amount, method, and timing must be decided directly between user and worker. In case of any payment issue or dispute, QuickKaam is not responsible.

4 Work Execution

Users must coordinate directly with the worker regarding work schedule, work quality, and completion time. QuickKaam does not guarantee work completion or quality.

5 Ticket & Service Requests

When a user raises a ticket, it will appear as Pending/Ongoing until accepted by a worker. Acceptance of tickets depends entirely on worker availability. QuickKaam does not guarantee that a ticket will be accepted.

6 Location & Contact Sharing

By raising a ticket, users agree to share their mobile number and location with workers. This information must be used only for service purposes.

7 Dispute Resolution

Any dispute regarding work, behavior, or payment must be resolved directly between user and worker. QuickKaam will not interfere in personal or financial disputes.

8 Account Deactivation

Users may deactivate their account anytime. After deactivation, you have 24 hours to login and cancel the deletion. After 24 hours, your account and data will be permanently removed.

  • Loss or damage during work
